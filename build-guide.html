Step-by-Step Assembly Guide

Take your time, follow safety precautions, and assemble your system methodically using the steps below.

Assembly Sequence

  1. Prepare the Motherboard: Install the CPU, M.2 NVMe SSD storage, and RAM modules while the motherboard is still resting on its cardboard box.
  2. Mount the CPU Cooler: Secure your air cooler or liquid cooling block bracket onto the CPU, applying thermal paste if not pre-applied.
  3. Install Power Supply & I/O Shield: Place your power supply unit into the bottom chamber of the case and snap the I/O shield into place (if not integrated).
  4. Install the Motherboard: Carefully lower the prepared motherboard into the chassis and secure it with standoff screws.
  5. Install the Graphics Card: Remove the necessary PCIe slot covers from the back of the case, slot the GPU into the top PCIe x16 slot until it clicks, and screw it down.
  6. Connect Cables & Cable Management: Plug in the 24-pin main power, 8-pin CPU power, front panel headers, and GPU power cables cleanly behind the motherboard tray.
