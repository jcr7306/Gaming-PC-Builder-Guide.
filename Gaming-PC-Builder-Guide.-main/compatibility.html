Hardware Compatibility Matrix

Ensuring your processor matches your motherboard socket and power supply unit is crucial to prevent hardware instability.

Processor & Motherboard Pairing Guide

Performance Tier Supported CPU Sockets Recommended RAM Type Minimum PSU Wattage
Entry Gaming LGA 1700 / AM4 DDR4-3200 550W - 650W
Mid-Range Performance LGA 1851 / AM5 DDR5-5600 750W
High-End Enthusiast LGA 1851 / AM5 DDR5-6000+ 850W - 1000W+

Always verify physical clearance dimensions with your chosen case, especially concerning long graphics cards and tall CPU air coolers.
